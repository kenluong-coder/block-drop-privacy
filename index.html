Block Drop – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how the Block Drop game ("the app") handles information. In short: the app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. It does not request internet access, so it cannot send any data off your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best (high) score locally on your device so it is still there the next time you play. This value never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it.

Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting data (for example, crash reporting), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
